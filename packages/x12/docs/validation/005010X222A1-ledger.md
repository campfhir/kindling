# X12 005010X222A1 837 Professional: loop and segment structure checked against free public sources

Researched 2026-10-30. Every URL below was downloaded during this session and its text checked (PDFs converted with `pdftotext -layout`). The CMS side-by-side was also cropped to its 5010 column with `pdftotext -x 345 -W 600`. The 837P part of the CMS Transmittal 1145 spreadsheet was cut to its first 134 columns, which hold the edit reference, segment/element, name, type, min-max, usage, loop, loop repeat and values.
Sources are limited to CMS, a Medicare MAC (CGS), state Medicaid publications (Nebraska, Ohio) and x12.org. No vendor, health plan or blog pages are used.
The full 005010X222A1 TR3 is sold by X12 and was **not** consulted. X12 Glass needs a paid license and was not used.

The 2300 loop sits under 2000B when there is no 2000C, and under 2000C when there is one. ED and XW give 2300 one loop ID wherever it occurs. That placement comes from reading HL04 together with the X12 examples (see below); the TR3's own wording on it was not available.

**HL nesting evidence:**
- HL03 codes: 2000A `20`, 2000B `22`, 2000C `23` (ED p.81, p.114; XW for 2000C).
- HL02: N/U in 2000A, R in 2000B and 2000C (ED, XW).
- HL04 values: 2000A `1`, 2000B `0, 1`, 2000C `0` (ED; XW for 2000C).
- Usage: 2000A R, 2000B R, 2000C **S** (ED p.142, XW). Every HL loop repeats `>1` (ED/XW loop rows).
- When 2000C exists: OH, on the 2000B HL (TR3 p.114): *"For Ohio Medicaid, the 'insured', 'subscriber' and the 'patient' are always the same person."* OH sets 2000B HL04 to `0 No subordinate HL segment in this hierarchical structure.` CGS (Table 6, TR3 p.142 and p.144) says the 2000C HL and PAT "Must not be present. For Medicare, the subscriber is always the same as the patient." CGS Table 13 sets 2000B HL04 = "0", and Table 14 sets SBR02 = `18` for the same reason. So 2000C is present only when the patient is a different person from the subscriber, and then the subscriber HL04 is `1`.
- The X12 examples agree:
  - X1 and X12: the subscriber HL has HL04 `1`, SBR02 is empty, and a 2000C HL follows (parent = the subscriber HL, HL04 `0`) with PAT01 `19`, then NM1 `QC`.
  - X2 and X4: the subscriber HL has HL04 `0`, SBR02 `18`, and there is no 2000C; CLM follows the 2010BB payer.
- HL04 code meaning `0` = "No subordinate HL segment in this hierarchical structure" is printed by OH. The meaning of `1` is **not printed** by any source used (see Could not confirm).
